About Olu-Sanu

A legacy of quality printing since 1979, serving Osun, Oyo, and Lagos States.

We Translate Your Imagination Into Reality

Olu-Sanu Global Limited is a subsidiary of Olu-Sanu Printing Press, a well-established printing services company based in Osun State, Oyo State, and Lagos State. We have been in business since 1979 and continue to grow steadily, reaching many cities in the 44+ years since our inception.

Over the years, we have evolved with our customers' needs to offer everything from offset printing to digital printing and everything in between.

We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, our ever-improving technology, and our technical and creative expertise. We use all this to ensure your printing is of the highest quality, helping you put your best foot forward when talking to prospects.

Our Services

From digital to offset, wide format to merchandise — we handle it all.

Digital Print

Short runs made fast and easy. Duplexing and personalization on a wide variety of in-house papers.

Offset Print

Medium to high volume with A2 and A1 modern offset equipment. UV technology for vibrant colour.

Wide Format & Signage

Posters, exhibition, neon signage, vehicle and building graphics. Indoor or outdoor options.

Prototype & Packaging

Kongsberg digital cutting table for packaging prototypes. Box development from start to finish.

Merchandise

Branded caps, T-shirts, pens, tote bags, keyrings, cups, and mugs. Premium promotional products.

Finishing

Die-cut, folded, stapled, PUR bound. Our passionate team takes care of your project until it's done.

Campaign Management

Planning multiple products for a mailing or event? We deliver seamless campaigns across all formats.

Mailing & Logistics

Packaging, labelling, freight, logistics, and storage of printed material. Service right to the end.

Graphic Design

Our experienced graphic designers can create anything from scratch or recreate your existing artwork.
